Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

ThinkTrail.AI helps students learn through guided explanations, practice, quizzes, and educational activities. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account or sign in, we may receive your email address and basic profile information from the authentication provider you choose. We may also store your learning activity, such as subjects, questions, progress, streaks, and points.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, provide the learning features, save your progress, improve the service, and protect the service from misuse.

Service providers

ThinkTrail.AI uses Supabase for authentication and data storage. If you choose Google, Microsoft, Apple, or another sign-in provider, that provider processes information according to its own privacy policy.

Children

ThinkTrail.AI is intended for students and should be used with a parent, guardian, or school where required. We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is needed to provide the service.

Data choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your account information. Contact the developer through the contact method provided with the service.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.
